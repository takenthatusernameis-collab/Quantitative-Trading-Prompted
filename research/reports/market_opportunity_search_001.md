# Report: Evidence-based market-opportunity search (market_opportunity_search_001)

**Cycle 947.** Read-only, deterministic. No walk-forward evaluation, no parameter search, no candidate implementation, no write under `data/` or `config/`, no fetcher call, no change to any committed record, gate, protocol or threshold.

This report is the long-form evidence behind the search record `research/experiments/market_opportunity_search_001.md`; both are generated by `research/market_opportunity_search_001.py` and contain no timestamp, so two consecutive runs are byte-identical.

## 1. Method and scope

1. **Inventory** every file under `data/raw/` (recursive): counts, date ranges, frequency, gaps, duplicate timestamps, non-positive closes, zero-volume rows, median dollar volume. Nothing is sampled and nothing is assumed.
2. **Re-measure the cost model** that is really implemented, in `src/backtest/engine.py`, and state the frictions it does **not** contain.
3. **Score eleven market segments** on the seven AGENTS.md dimensions. (a), (d), (e) and the *hurdle* half of (c) are rule-based over measured quantities; (b), (f) and the *gross* half of (c) are **labelled declared priors**. (c) is published as two numbers and scored by their minimum.
4. Run **one** pre-declared feasibility screen on the segment the scoring leaves open, and publish its result whatever it is.
5. Publish an explicit, evidence-based rejection reason for **every** segment, so that the next cycle inherits evidence instead of guesses.

**No closed line is re-scored and no closed line is re-opened.** The status of every earlier candidate is quoted verbatim from its committed record and the quote is verified to be present in that record.

## 2. The cost model that is really available

| Component | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Commission | 1.0 bp per unit of weight change | `src/backtest/engine.py::get_cost_params` -> `config/settings.yaml` |
| Slippage | 2.0 bp per unit of weight change | `src/backtest/engine.py::get_cost_params` -> `config/settings.yaml` |
| **Total per unit of weight change** | **3.0 bp** | derived |
| Risk-free rate | 2.00%/yr | `src/backtest/engine.py::get_risk_free_rate` |

**The frictions this model does NOT contain:** `borrow`, `impact`, `spread`, `latency`, `margin`. A candidate whose mechanism needs one of them is penalised on (a), (c) and (f); a candidate whose mechanism needs one of them and cannot be measured from the cache is **blocked**, not merely expensive.

**The uniform cost hurdle used for the (c) score.** A monthly, long-only rule that replaces its whole book at every rebalance pays 24 units of weight change per year, i.e. at most **0.7200%/yr** at 3.0 bp/unit. This is an upper bound on the annual drag of *any* monthly long-only rule in the repository, and the (c) hurdle is that bound expressed as a share of the segment's own measured annualised volatility: a segment whose rules must capture a large share of their own risk scale to pay their costs is a bad host, whatever its prior.

## 4. The pre-declared feasibility screen

**Declared before the screen was computed, from the inventory and the literature prior only; no parameter of it is selected by its result, and it was not changed after the screen was read.**

- Candidate id: `commoditytrend_001` (a *screen*, not a pre-registered line)
- Universe: DBC, GLD -- DBC and GLD are the only two commodity exposures in the committed cache with enough cached daily history to support the frozen 10y/12m/6m walk-forward design; the 2015-01-02 common start of the other four commodity ETFs supports three folds only
- Rule: absolute time-series momentum: hold every asset whose trailing 252-session total return, skipping the most recent 21 sessions, is positive; equal weight 1/N over the held assets; 100% cash when none is held
- Signal: trailing 252-session total return skipping the most recent 21 sessions, formed on the month-end session
- Rebalance: month_end with a 1-session signal lag
- Benchmark: 1/N buy-and-hold of the same universe over the same sessions

**This screen is in-sample, full-sample and non-selective.** It is not a walk-forward evaluation, it produces no fold statistics, and it cannot support any promotion or rejection claim. It answers one question: does the rule capture any gross edge at all over its own benchmark, once the repository's 3.0 bp per unit of weight change is paid?

**The screen's result, published whatever it is.** The rule's net benchmark-relative excess is **-0.5304%/yr**. It is negative, so the pre-declared screen **does not support** this candidate: the rule does not capture a benchmark-relative gross edge that its own (tiny, 0.0683%/yr) cost drag does not already remove, and the rule's absolute net Sharpe of 0.4041 is below the repository's 0.9 promotion gate. Two independent facts therefore stand against this instantiation: the **edge** (a -0.5304%/yr benchmark-relative excess) and the **trade count** (45 sessions with a weight change in 5201 sessions, i.e. a monthly single-book rule that changes its position in 58 of 249 rebalances, so the pooled out-of-sample trade count cannot clear the >100 gate).

**What the screen does and does not settle.** It settles the *two-asset* instantiation on the 2006-2026 panel: absolute time-series momentum on {DBC, GLD} has no benchmark-relative edge there. It does **not** settle the commodity *segment*, because the diversified form of the mechanism (six commodity exposures rather than two) cannot be built on the committed cache at all -- see section 5.

## 7. Disposition

**No candidate is pre-registered by this cycle, and the reason is published rather than smoothed over.** The selection rule leaves exactly one segment open -- `commodity_complex`, the commodity complex, the only segment the repository has never traded as a book -- and its only instantiation that the committed cache can actually evaluate fails the pre-declared screen of section 4 on both the edge and the trade count. The remaining ten segments are closed by committed records, by the absence of the data their mechanism needs, or by the repository's own cost model; each one's reason is published in section 6.

The **conditional candidate specification** below is published so that the next cycle does not have to re-derive it, and so that the specification can be frozen and evaluated without modification once -- and only once -- its **data gate** is satisfied. It is **not** a pre-registration: no hypothesis file and no configuration file is written for it by this cycle, and no result of it exists.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `commoditytrend_001` |
| mechanism | Absolute (time-series) momentum inside the commodity complex: a commodity price that is in a 12-month uptrend is expected to keep paying its trend premium (documented time-series momentum in commodity futures); a commodity price in a 12-month downtrend is expected to keep paying its downtrend premium, so the book is flat in cash rather than short |
| direction | long the assets in an uptrend, flat in cash otherwise |
| horizon | monthly rebalance, 252-session signal with a 21-session skip |
| universe | DBC, BNO, UNG, GLD |
| benchmark | 1/N buy-and-hold of the same universe |
| cost model | 3.0 bp per unit of weight change (1.0 bp commission + 2.0 bp slippage, src/backtest/engine.py) at a 2.00% risk-free rate; long-only, so no borrow; the embedded ETF expense ratio and the embedded roll yield of the commodity ETFs are NOT measurable from the cache and are declared blocked |
| data gate | the cached common-inception panel must support at least 15 folds of the frozen 10y/12m/6m design (>= 4,536 business days) |
| falsification | 1) walk-forward mean OOS Sharpe < 0.9; 2) the benchmark-relative net excess has a moving-block bootstrap 95% CI that includes zero; 3) the measured gross capture fraction is below the measured cost hurdle; 4) the positive-fold rate < 0.5 or the pooled OOS trades <= 100; 5) no positive benchmark-relative excess after 2010; 6) the data gate fails (the candidate is then blocked by data/infrastructure, not rejected) |

**The data gate, measured.** The frozen design needs 4,536 business days (18.00 years) to produce 15 folds. The four-instrument universe (DBC, BNO, UNG, GLD) has a cached common inception of 2015-01-02, which is **3 folds** -- the data gate fails today by a factor of 5.0. The two-instrument universe that section 4 could evaluate (DBC, GLD) has 21 folds and does clear the gate, and its screen is negative.
