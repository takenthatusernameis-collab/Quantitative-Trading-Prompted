## Cycle 938 — Pre-register and evaluate `liquidity_001` (Amihud illiquidity tilt, long-only)

### Context
Cycle 934 shortlisted four structurally distinct candidates on the committed nine-sector panel. The fixed order of execution was: `reversal_001` (28/35, rank 1) — executed/pre-registered and evaluated in Cycles 935/936, **Rejected/Failed** (Cycle 935 step 2). `pairs_001` (25/35, rank 2) — pre-registered and evaluated in Cycle 936, **Rejected/Failed** (Cycle 936). `liquidity_001` is next at **24/35** (rank 3). `leadlag_001` is rank 4 (16/35).

`liquidity_001` (Amihud illiquidity tilt, long-only): score each sector ETF by Amihud measure |21-session return| / dollar volume over trailing 21 sessions; hold the most illiquid quintile (2 of 9) at equal weight, cash otherwise; rebalance monthly (every ~21 sessions) with signal formed at t effective at t+1; benchmark 1/N equal-weight of the same nine sectors; cost model 3 bp/unit weight change (24 units/yr turnover -> 0.7200%/yr). Known confounders include volume-denominated ranking, volume spikes inverting signal, ETF volume fragmentation, and embedded expense ratios/tracking differences. Falsification conditions: (i) WF mean OOS Sharpe < 0.9; (ii) benchmark-relative net excess bootstrap 95% CI includes zero; (iii) measured cross-sectional dispersion of Amihud score too small to cover cost hurdle; (iv) net excess not positive post-2009.

Universe is the same nine GICS sector ETFs (XLB,XLE,XLF,XLI,XLK,XLP,XLU,XLV,XLY) with identical cached history (6991 sessions, 1998-12-22 .. 2026-10-08) as used by reversal_001 and pairs_001.

### Objective

Publish benchmark-relative results vs 1/N of the same nine sectors, measured turnover/cost drag, bootstrap CI on benchmark-relative excess, positive-fold rate, pooled trades, Deflated Sharpe Ratio over declared trial count, the falsification checks against the pre-registered conditions, and a clear verdict (Promotable/NO-GO or Rejected/Failed).
