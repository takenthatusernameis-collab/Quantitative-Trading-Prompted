`pairs_001` as published in the shortlist:

- **Family / mechanism**: statistical arbitrage (spread convergence). Two securities that share a common driver trade with a temporary dispersion around their long-run relationship; the dispersion reverts, and a market-neutral book (long the cheap leg, short the rich leg) earns the convergence without the common factor.
- **Universe**: the nine GICS sector ETFs XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLU, XLV, XLY — 6,991 identical sessions, 1998-12-22 .. 2026-10-08, 0 missing closes, 0 duplicate timestamps. Median instrument dollar volume $296,232,152/day.
- **The six pre-registered pairs** (fixed by the shortlist, and to be frozen verbatim in the candidate's own config): **(XLP, XLU), (XLF, XLK), (XLE, XLB), (XLI, XLB), (XLV, XLY), (XLK, XLU)**.
- **Baseline rule**: z-score the log price ratio over the trailing **126** sessions; take the contrarian side when **|z| >= 1**; **dollar-neutral per pair**; rebalance every **5th session**; the signal formed at `t` is effective at **`t+1`**.
- **Holding horizon**: 5 sessions (52 rebalances per year).
- **Benchmark**: cash (a market-neutral book); the repository's 2.00% annual risk-free rate is the hurdle.
- **Cost model**: the repository's committed 3.0 bp per unit of weight change (1.0 bp commission + 2.0 bp slippage, `config/settings.yaml`); **no borrow or short-side cost exists anywhere in `config/` or `data/`**, so the borrow assumption is pre-registered in the candidate's own config (below).
- **Measured inputs published by the shortlist**: 1,398 non-overlapping holding-horizon observations, 4.96 effective independent bets, measured gross scale **69.6626%/yr** (median |5-session change| of the pairs' log price ratio x 52 rebalances), cost hurdle **2.2394%** of that scale under the (now corrected) proxy turnover, shortlist score 25/35.
- **Known confounders** (published in the shortlist): (1) short-sale constraints and recall risk are unmodelled — the repository has no borrow cost at all; (2) pair-selection multiplicity — six pre-registered pairs whose spread correlations are high, so the six bets are far from independent; (3) structural breaks in the long-run relationship turn convergence into a value trap; (4) the market-neutral construction must be funded and pays the 2% risk-free rate, which the canonical Sharpe convention does not net.
- **Falsification** (published in the shortlist; any one falsifies): (i) the walk-forward mean OOS Sharpe < 0.9; (ii) the net excess over cash has a moving-block bootstrap 95% CI that includes zero after deflating for the six pairs tested; (iii) the cointegration of any pair fails out of sample; (iv) the net excess does not survive a borrow cost of 25 bp per unit of short notional per rebalance.

Two specific carry-overs from Cycle 935's evidence:

1. **The borrow assumption is pre-registered in the candidate's own config, not in the shared cost model.** `config/settings.yaml` is **not** modified.
