# Cycle 939 — Pre-register and evaluate `leadlag_001` (futures-to-ETF delayed price discovery)

## Context
Cycle 934 shortlisted four structurally distinct candidates on committed data. Execution order: `reversal_001` (Cycle 935/936, Rejected/Failed), `pairs_001` (Cycle 936, Rejected/Failed), `liquidity_001` (Cycle 938, Rejected/Failed — WF mean OOS Sharpe -0.649990; conditions i, iv fired). `leadlag_001` is the remaining shortlist candidate at **16/35** (rank 4 of 4). All three evaluated candidates are Rejected/Failed; the incumbent `tsmomentum_spytrend_001` stays Credible Candidate / NO-GO.

`leadlag_001` (futures-to-ETF delayed price discovery, long-only ETF leg, cash otherwise): for each of five pre-registered futures-to-ETF pairs, take the sign of the futures contract's trailing 5-session return as the signal for the ETF leg; long the ETF when positive, cash otherwise; rebalance every 5th session (52/yr), signal at `t` effective at `t+1`; benchmark = cash; cost model 3 bp/unit (52 units/yr -> 1.5600%/yr). Known confounders: front-month roll price unobservable (return series contaminated at every roll; roll dates are ex-post); CL-F non-positive close inside the sample (went negative in 2020); futures/ETF calendars differ (paired panel complete only on sessions both markets open); daily bar cannot establish intraday ordering (weaker next-session form only); five pairs are not independent bets (four different factor exposures). Falsification conditions: (i) WF mean OOS Sharpe < 0.9; (ii) net excess over cash moving-block bootstrap 95% CI includes zero after DSR deflation for the five pairs; (iii) excess disappears once detected roll windows are excluded (roll artefact); (iv) excess carried by CL-F window (non-positive close makes sample unusable).

Universe: CL-F, GC-F, NG-F, ZB-F (futures) x USO, BNO, UNG, GSG, GLD, TLT (ETFs) — all committed parquet cache files exist in `data/raw/`. Data caveats scored 1/5 on (a): coverage 11.8y (2015-01-02 .. 2026-10-15), cross-section not constant (51.5% of sessions), one integrity defect row in a required series, roll price/cost absent. With 11.8y of data a 10y train leaves little test span — a real statistical-power limitation disclosed in the hypothesis, not a reason to alter the design.
